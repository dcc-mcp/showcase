How to read these

A picture of a render proves very little on its own. Each work is meant to be read in three passes.

01 — LOOK AT THE OUTPUT

Judge the frames first. If the result is not something you would want, nothing below it matters.

02 — CHECK THE DRIVER

Ask what actually produced it: a real host process, an adapter imported inside that host, or the host's own scripting API. These are different claims and only one of them is a claim about DCC-MCP.

03 — READ WHAT FAILED

Works marked gap found exist because something did not work. The failures are recorded because they are the part worth transferring.

Every image on this page was screened for embedded text before publication to keep internal paths, hosts and identifiers out of the gallery. Works without a full report are labelled as such rather than given one retroactively.
