# Asset rights and provenance

The bundled generated-model GLBs are derivatives of real Hyper3D/Rodin API
outputs. Prop reference blockouts were authored procedurally for this work;
the courier and mechanical guardian use approved original ImageGen concepts.
`assets/derivative-provenance.json` records original and derivative hashes,
conversion, texture sizes, native reimport checks and external-resource checks.
Original provider downloads are preserved separately; this game bundles only
the game derivatives. Prop display fitting does not establish provider source
units, physical accuracy, uniqueness or exclusivity. The character derivatives
use authored coordinate normalization, skins and animation; their source UVs
and embedded PBR texture payloads are retained. See `assets/asset-map.json` for
the selected originals, derivative hashes and authorship.

Hyper3D output use is governed by its [Terms of Service](https://hyper3d.ai/legal/terms),
reviewed on 2026-10-24, including section 5(b), account/plan-specific conditions
and restrictions on prohibited, infringing or harmful use. These models are not
relicensed as MIT assets. This showcase grants no extra rights and makes no
claim of endorsement by Hyper3D or Deemos. Retain this notice and the provenance
when redistributing this project.

Original game code and the native UI follow the repository's MIT license.
Generated content retains its separate terms. Kenney-derived controller flow
retains the included MIT notice and source attribution. No Kenney visual art
is bundled. The renamed Relic Sans
font derives from Noto Sans CJK SC, retains SIL OFL 1.1 and includes source/output
hashes. Its original metadata copyright notice is preserved in the font. Godot
retains its MIT license and third-party notices in the shared runtime directory.

The native foundation, paths, hidden collision proxies and gameplay effects
are authored Godot geometry; they are not claimed as Hyper3D output. Visible
character surfaces are provider geometry/PBR with Blender-authored rigs and
clips. `TAPose` did not supply skeletons or animation.
The interactive chest partitions the provider exterior at its lid seam; its
hinge, interior lining and underside cap are authored derivatives. The original
closed chest remains preserved separately.

The six unmodified sound effects in `audio/` come from Kenney's RPG Audio 1.0
under CC0 1.0 Universal. `audio/AUDIO-SOURCES.json` records the pack source,
original member names and hashes; `KENNEY-RPG-AUDIO.txt` retains its license.
No third-party franchise assets, music or paid reference art are bundled.

## Reused equipment and vegetation

The separate sword and grove tree are KayKit assets by Kay Lousberg, licensed
CC0-1.0, reused from the existing licensed Showcase cache. They are not original
Hyper3D output or evidence that a generated mesh was automatically split.
`assets/reused/SOURCE.json` records input/output hashes and the byte-preserving
selection/repacking steps; the two original license texts are retained here.
The character skins and original animations remain unchanged in their GLBs.
The independent Run resource is adapted at runtime from the authored Walk keys
with a distinct duration and arm/torso poses; it is not a provider animation.
