- HTML source inspection and HEAD requests succeeded with standard TLS certificate verification. No video bytes were downloaded by this rights-research step. Technical metadata from filenames/player labels is advertised, not independently decoded. / 已通过默认 TLS 证书校验读取官方 HTML 并完成 HEAD 核验；此权利研究步骤未下载视频字节；文件名和播放器标签仅为公布规格。
