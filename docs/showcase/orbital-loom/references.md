# Orbital Loom — reference research (11 October 2026)

A brief current-reference pass before modeling:
- Studio Tobias Klein, Orbital Oscillation (December 2025): https://www.kleintobias.com/2512-orbital-oscillation . Slow controlled orbital movement and deliberate material restraint inform the pacing only; geometry, mechanism, and styling here are original.
- Shaul Tzemach, The Orbit Driver (2025): https://shaultzemach.com/the-orbit-driver/ . Search-visible description foregrounds bearings and an electronic mechanism; no source imagery or geometry is copied.
- Blender official Extra Mesh Objects 0.4.1 (1 April 2026), https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/extra-mesh-objects/versions/ . Free GPL-3+ community extension, Blender 4.2+. Considered for mechanical primitives; native meshes are sufficient and reduce dependencies.
- Official driver workflow: https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/animation/drivers/index.html . Native expression drivers and Track To constraint provide inspectable live motion.
- Official DCC adapter / agent skill: https://github.com/dcc-mcp/dcc-mcp-blender . Use the existing verified adapter once its current environment is confirmed.

Original direction: a small museum-quality tabletop gyroscopic loom in deep petrol enamel, satin brass, and ivory ceramic. Three counter-rotating hoops expose their shafts and bearings over a radial calibrated plinth. A continuous camera move reveals mechanism and silhouette. This is a motion visualization, not a claim of a fabrication-ready engineering design.
