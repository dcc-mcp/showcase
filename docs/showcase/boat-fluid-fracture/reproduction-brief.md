## Active source and production plan

Extract [source-intact.zip](source-intact.zip) with its relative structure intact. [SOURCE-INTACT.md](SOURCE-INTACT.md) and [source-intact-manifest.json](source-intact-manifest.json) record unchanged source geometry, UVs/normals, all five original texture maps, material boundaries, hashes and provider terms. OBJ parsing measured 500000 triangle faces; this is a source count, not a new fracture result. A new destination import/save/reopen test on this delivered intact archive is still required.

Preserve the source, measure native bounds/units and explicitly document any solid/volume preparation. Use the installed Houdini RBD Material Fracture wood workflow and record actual grain settings, cut detail, piece counts, UV/interior treatment and constraints. Continue through Bullet and real FLIP coupling with actual per-piece mass/density/volume, collision proxies and sampled loading. Constraint and force views must expose measured native values and units; illustrative colors alone cannot prove coupling.

Use an elevated camera that contains the rising/outward liquid plume and actual local wood break front, with the critical failure region visible through the blast. Deliver an intact native view, fracture close-up, constraint/force view, collision guides, mass table, impact sequence and post-impact debris/waterline evidence. A hidden-water diagnostic is useful only when labeled; it does not prove flotation. Target24fps consecutive native frames; actual cache range, fps, duration and native recipe counts remain unverified until measured. A separate whitewater pass can add foam/spray/bubbles from cached fluid fields after primary native acceptance.

Follow the local waterline and actual released-mesh motion through a declared contiguous settling interval, with visible and collision-proxy bounds clear of floor/walls. Save/reopen the final native result, reopen a delivered export, rebuild independently from the actual new recipe and compare declared tolerances. Inspect real consecutive frames and normal-speed video before final acceptance; retain the measured solver timing and correct color pipeline. Browser playback requires the official DCC-CUA exact-bound route, whose current browser launch remains blocked.
